Farm management software

One dashboard for the whole field record

The software layer that ties drone scans, sensor data, irrigation, and treatments to each field. Some of this ships today; some is still being built — each card below says which.

Available

Farm Dashboard

Overall status across your fields — moisture, nutrients, alerts, and device health in one view.

In development

Field Maps

Satellite/boundary map view per field, with drone-derived overlays once scanning is complete for that field.

In development

Drone Scan History

Every scan stored and compared against the previous one for the same field.

Available

Alerts

Threshold-based notifications for moisture, flow, pressure, and device status.

Available

Sensor History

Soil moisture, temperature, and EC charted over time, by zone.

Available

Irrigation Records

Cycle duration, water volume, and zone-level irrigation history.

Planned

Treatment Records

Fertilizer, nutrient, pesticide, and fungicide applications logged by field, zone, and date.

Planned

Reports

Downloadable per-field reports combining scans, sensor data, and treatment history.

Available

Device Management

Status and connectivity for every node, gateway, and controller on the farm.

This page describes the intended shape of the IrrigTech software platform. Cards marked Available reflect the current sensor and irrigation dashboard; cards marked In development or Planned are design targets, not yet shipped.
