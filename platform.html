Honest positioning

What's available today, and what's ahead

IrrigTech is being built in stages. Here's what each capability actually is right now.

Available

Monitor

Field sensors, water and irrigation monitoring, and farm input records — IrrigTech's current hardware and dashboard, available through a project consultation.

Prototype

Scan

Drone-based field mapping and crop imagery. Hardware specification is defined; field testing is ongoing.

In development

Analyze

AI-assisted crop and stress analytics from sensor and imagery data. Not yet validated for commercial decision-making on its own.

In development

Predict

Trend and yield intelligence, where the underlying data has been validated for a given crop and region.

Planned

Automate

Expanded irrigation and treatment automation workflows, building on today's manual and threshold-based controls.

Working with IrrigTech

How a pilot program runs

New capabilities are introduced on a limited number of fields before wider deployment.

1

Assessment

We review your fields, crops, and what you're trying to learn or solve.

2

System design

We propose which hardware and monitoring makes sense for your site.

3

Limited deployment

Installation on a defined set of fields or zones, not the whole farm at once.

4

Baseline collection

A period of monitoring establishes what "normal" looks like for your fields.

5

Drone / sensor monitoring

Ongoing scans and readings build the field record through the season.

6

Results review

We go through what the data showed, together, before scaling up.

7

Farm-wide deployment

Once validated on the pilot fields, the system extends across the farm.
