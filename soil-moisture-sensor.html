Soil Moisture Sensor Node

Accurate soil insights, smarter irrigation — a focused, lower-cost probe for farms that just need reliable moisture data at scale.

Where the IoT Sensor Node covers moisture, EC, pH and air conditions all at once, the Soil Moisture Sensor Node does one thing at the lowest possible cost per point: track soil moisture and temperature continuously, so you can afford to place far more of them across a field and see variation between rows, not just one reading per zone.
