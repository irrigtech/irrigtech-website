About IrrigTech

We think soil deserves the same visibility as everything else on the farm

Weather gets watched hourly. Soil gets tested once a season, if that. We built the hardware and software to close that gap.

IrrigTech is an engineering project by Pak-EL LAB, currently at prototype and early-deployment stage rather than a live commercial product. It started with a simple frustration: by the time a plant visibly shows a problem, the yield hit has usually already happened. Lab soil tests are accurate but slow. We build field hardware that reads the same signals continuously, cheaply enough to leave in the ground all season — and we're extending that same idea upward, from soil sensors to drone field scanning and a shared digital record for every field. See the full platform for where that's headed, or the how it works page for what's built today versus planned.
