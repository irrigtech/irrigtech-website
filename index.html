FAQ

Common questions

Is the Field Scanner Drone available to buy today?

No — it's currently a prototype. Field sensors, valve/pump controllers, and the field gateway are IrrigTech's current hardware, available through a project consultation.

What does "AI-assisted analysis" actually mean?

It means imagery and sensor data are processed to flag anomalies and patterns worth a closer look — decision support, not an automatic diagnosis. Ground verification is recommended before acting on any flagged area.

Can the drone diagnose plant disease or exact nutrient levels?

No. RGB, multispectral, and thermal imagery can indicate stress patterns worth investigating, but they can't reliably diagnose a specific disease or measure exact nutrient concentrations on their own.

Does IrrigTech work without reliable internet in the field?

Field nodes and gateways are designed to store readings locally and forward them once a connection is available, rather than losing data during a signal gap.

What happens if I get an automation threshold wrong?

Automated irrigation acts only on the thresholds you configure. You can review and adjust them at any time, and manual control is always available.
