Pump Controller

Smart control, reliable performance — automates pump operations, monitors performance in real time, and protects your system.

The Pump Controller watches over the pump itself — starting and stopping it remotely, tracking pressure, current and runtime, and cutting power the moment something looks wrong. Dry-run, overload, and over/under-voltage protection are built in, so a bad connection or an empty tank doesn't turn into a burned-out motor.
