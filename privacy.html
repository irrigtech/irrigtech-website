Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

IrrigTech, by Pak-EL LAB, is currently a prototype/early-deployment engineering project. This website does not have user accounts, a public dashboard login, or live device telemetry today — the only personal data this site collects is what you submit through the contact form below, plus standard hosting logs.

What this covers

This policy explains what IrrigTech collects when you use this website, and how that information is used, stored, and shared. It will be expanded to cover dashboard and field-hardware data once those are live, billable parts of the service.

Information we collect today

  • Contact form submissions — the name, email, company, phone, and project details you enter on the Contact page. These are submitted via Netlify Forms and delivered to our team by email.
  • Direct messages — anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Basic hosting logs — standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) kept by our hosting provider, Netlify, for security and reliability.

We do not currently operate user accounts, a public dashboard, or a system that ingests live field-sensor data from customer hardware. When those parts of the platform go live, this policy will be updated first, and it will describe exactly what is collected (for pilot deployments we design that per project, in writing, before any device reports data).

How we use it

Contact form and direct-message details are used only to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, to run that project. We don't sell this information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep contact-form submissions and related correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you or deliver a project, and then delete or archive them.

Sharing

We share data with sub-processors only where needed to operate this site and respond to you — currently our hosting provider (Netlify) and, if you message us there, WhatsApp/Meta — under their own terms of service.

Security

Contact form submissions are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We don't ask for passwords, payment details, or other sensitive credentials through this site.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@irrigtech.com

Final legal wording — including data-retention periods and sub-processor list — should be reviewed and confirmed by the site owner before this policy is relied upon.
