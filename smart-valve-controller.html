Smart Valve Controller

Intelligent control, efficient irrigation — precise automation of irrigation valves for optimal water usage and healthier crops.

The Smart Valve Controller sits right at the manifold and does one job well: open and close irrigation valves exactly when they should. Trigger it on a fixed schedule, by weather, or automatically from an IoT Sensor Node's readings — either way, it runs on battery or solar across multiple seasons on a single charge cycle and reports every action back to the dashboard over LoRa.
